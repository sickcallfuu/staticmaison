Added a link under comics to this old comic I made back in 2012. I think this was my first self-published zine. It's about 40pp and is over at the [NY Cartoonists Library Digital Archive](https://nycartoonlibrary.org/margotspider.html). There's a lot of older self-published comics over here, it's really cool and definitely worth checking out!
