## miss-canny
I've got a comic in the upcoming Ex.Mag vol 7, which is a really nice anthology published by [Peow](https://peow.studio). It's being funded on [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/peow/exmag-volume-7?ref=creator_tab) right now if you're interested in doing a pre-order. They've already reached the goal but there are some cool extras/rewards you can pick up.

My comic is 58 pages and is called Miss Canny. I added a section for it under comics on this site and posted the first 16 pages. I'll post the rest after the book has been published and out for a while but that probably won't be until next year.

***~ [Miss Canny - excerpt](/comics/miss-canny) ~***

I tried formatting this section differently... I've just been posting all comics pages on one page, which feels kind of not ideal for longer comics. With this one, I put each image on its own page and you can either click the current image to advance to the next page or use the navigation at the bottom of the page. It might be kind of fussy right now but I want to try to do the same for the other longer comics on this site. I also did this in a way that was really inefficient... I know it's possible to generate pages in Hugo based on a directory of images or different files so I'll have to look into that... (I did something with content adaptors for this [gallery of screenshots](https://fuu.margot.maison/teedna/shadowland-alone/) on my Fromsoft site but I sort of just copied a lot from the repo linked in [this thread](https://discourse.gohugo.io/t/create-pages-based-on-images-in-the-assets-directory/53956/2) and didn't have a good understanding of what I was actually doing.)
